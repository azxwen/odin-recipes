How to make Rice

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Water
  • Any seasonings of your choice

Steps

  1. Scoop rice with your measurement cup and place it into rice bowl cooker.
  2. Wash rice in water at least three times or until water becomes clear.
  3. Fill up water to the measurement of cups of rice.
  4. Put rice bowl in rice cooker.
  5. Plug in rice cooker and press the tab to "cook" to turn on rice cooker.
